# Grammar Quest

An interactive K-5 grammar and English language mechanics learning application designed for an international school serving English Language Learners.

## Master Standards Reference

The full K-5 grammar standards document is at `docs/k5-grammar-standards.md`. It synthesizes CCSS, IXL, TEKS, and major curriculum publishers into one unified grade-by-grade reference. **Always consult this document when developing skills, exercises, or grade-level content.**

### Grade-Level Domains (each grade covers all of these)
- Parts of speech (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, conjunctions, prepositions, articles)
- Sentence structure
- Capitalization
- Punctuation
- Spelling patterns and rules
- Usage
- Vocabulary and word study

### Key Skill Progression Summary
- **K**: Common nouns, action verbs, oral adjectives, simple pronouns, basic prepositions, question words, complete sentences (oral), end punctuation, CVC spelling
- **Grade 1**: Proper/possessive nouns, past/present/future verbs, adjectives, personal/possessive/indefinite pronouns, conjunctions (and/but/or), articles/demonstratives, 4 sentence types, commas in dates/series, digraphs/blends/VCe
- **Grade 2**: Collective nouns, irregular plurals, irregular past tense, adjectives vs. adverbs, reflexive pronouns, subordinating conjunctions, compound sentences, apostrophes, all 6 syllable types
- **Grade 3**: Abstract nouns, regular/irregular verbs (formal), comparative/superlative adj/adv, pronoun-antecedent agreement, simple/compound/complex sentences, dialogue punctuation, Latin roots
- **Grade 4**: Progressive tenses, modals (can/may/must), adjective order, relative pronouns/adverbs, prepositional phrases, fragments/run-ons correction, comma in compound sentences, Greek/Latin affixes
- **Grade 5**: Perfect tenses, tense shift correction, correlative conjunctions, independent/dependent clauses, interjections, comma after introductory elements, titles formatting, advanced Greek/Latin roots

### ELL Considerations
- Articles, SVO order, and verb tenses need explicit sustained instruction
- BICS ~2 years, CALP 5-7+ years
- Use sentence frames, mentor sentences, and visual supports at every level
- Newcomer ELL sequence: pronouns -> "to be" -> verbs vs. nouns -> present tense -> singular/plural -> articles -> demonstratives -> possessives -> progressive -> negatives -> questions -> adjectives -> comparatives -> quantifiers -> past -> future -> modals -> adverbs -> conjunctions -> prepositions
