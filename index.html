🔢 Error Rules

Each sentence contains exactly 5 errors to find and correct.

🏷️ Parts of Speech Order (Hardcoded)

POS are presented in this strict sequence:

1️⃣ All Nouns (left to right)

2️⃣ All Verbs (left to right)

3️⃣ All Adjectives (left to right)

4️⃣ All Adverbs (left to right)

5️⃣ All Prepositions (left to right)

6️⃣ All Others (pronouns, conjunctions, etc.)

Parts of Speech Labels

N = Noun   V = Verb   ADJ = Adjective

PRO = Pronoun   PREP = Preposition   ADV = Adverb

CONJ = Conjunction (FANBOYS)   ART = Article   DEM = Demonstrative

SUBCONJ = Subordinating Conjunction (because, when, if, although, while)

OBJPRO = Object Pronoun (me, him, her, us, them)

POSS = Possessive   PP = Past Participle   MODAL = Modal Verb
