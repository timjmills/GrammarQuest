# Grade 2 Curriculum Outline — 150 Days (6 Units × 25 Days)

## Key Grade 2 Standards (CCSS + TEKS + IXL)
- Collective nouns (group, herd, crowd, flock, team)
- Irregular plural nouns (feet, children, teeth, mice, fish, geese, men, women)
- Irregular past tense verbs (sat, hid, told, ran, gave, made, came, saw)
- Adjectives vs. adverbs (choosing correctly based on what is modified)
- Reflexive pronouns (myself, yourself, himself, herself, ourselves, themselves)
- Compound sentences (produce, expand, rearrange)
- Capitalize holidays, product names, geographic names
- Commas in greetings/closings of letters, in a series, in dates
- Apostrophes for contractions and possessives
- Prefixes: un-, re-, dis-
- Compound words
- Synonyms, antonyms, homophones
- Shades of meaning (verbs and adjectives)
- Subject-verb agreement
- Fragments and run-ons identification

Strategic review of Grade 1: common/proper nouns, action verbs, past/present/future tenses, articles (a/an/the), demonstratives, sentence types (statement/question/command/exclamation), end punctuation, commas in dates/series
