# Grade 3 Curriculum Outline — 150 Days (6 Units × 25 Days)

## Key Grade 3 Standards (CCSS + TEKS + IXL)
- Abstract nouns (childhood, courage, freedom)
- Regular and irregular plural nouns
- Regular and irregular verbs; simple verb tenses (past, present, future)
- Comparative and superlative adjectives and adverbs
- Pronoun-antecedent agreement
- Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS) and subordinating conjunctions
- Simple, compound, and complex sentences
- Commas in addresses, dialogue, and quotation marks
- Possessive nouns (singular and plural)
- Capitalize titles, proper adjectives, nationalities, languages
- Latin roots: port, form, ject, spect, dict
- Distinguish literal vs. nonliteral language
- Shades of meaning

Strategic review of Grades 1-2: common/proper nouns, action/helping verbs, articles, subject/predicate, end punctuation, irregular plurals

## Unit 1: Foundations & Review (Days 1-25)
**Theme:** Building a Strong Grammar Base

| Days | Skill Focus | Details |
|------|------------|---------|
| 1-3 | Common & proper nouns review | Person/place/thing/idea, capitalization |
| 4-6 | Singular & plural nouns (regular) | -s, -es, -ies rules |
| 7-9 | Irregular plural nouns | children, mice, teeth, geese, feet, men, women |
| 10-12 | Action verbs & helping verbs | Main verb + helper (is, am, are, was, were, has, have, had) |
| 13-15 | Simple tenses review | Past (-ed), present, future (will) |
| 16-18 | Subject & predicate | Complete/simple subject and predicate identification |
| 19-21 | Articles & demonstratives review | a/an/the, this/that/these/those |
| 22-23 | Sentence types review | Statement, question, command, exclamation |
| 24-25 | Unit 1 Review | Mixed foundational skills |

**Vocab themes:** Character traits, school, natural world
