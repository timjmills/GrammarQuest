# Grade 4 Curriculum Outline — 150 Days (6 Units × 25 Days)

## Key Grade 4 Standards (CCSS + TEKS + IXL)
- Progressive verb tenses (was walking, am walking, will be walking)
- Modal auxiliaries (can, may, must, should, would, could)
- Adjective ordering (a small red bag, not a red small bag)
- Relative pronouns (who, whose, whom, which, that)
- Relative adverbs (where, when, why)
- Prepositional phrases
- Fragments and run-ons correction
- Comma before coordinating conjunction in compound sentences
- Quotation marks in dialogue
- Frequently confused words (to/too/two, there/their, its/it's, your/you're)
- Greek and Latin affixes and roots
- Similes, metaphors, idioms, adages, proverbs
- Capitalize historical events, periods, documents, titles of works
- Subject-verb agreement with compound subjects

Strategic review of Grade 2-3: irregular plurals, simple tenses, comparative/superlative adj/adv, subordinating conjunctions, possessive nouns

## Unit 1: Foundations & Review (Days 1-25)
**Theme:** Reviewing Grade 3 skills and building the bridge to Grade 4

| Days | Skill Focus | Details |
|------|------------|---------|
| 1-3 | Irregular plurals & possessives review | children/teeth/mice/geese, singular & plural possessives |
| 4-6 | Simple tenses review + subject-verb agreement | past/present/future, compound subjects |
| 7-9 | Comparative & superlative adj/adv | -er/-est, more/most, irregular forms (good/better/best) |
| 10-12 | Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS) | and, but, or, so, yet + compound sentences |
| 13-15 | Subordinating conjunctions review | because, when, if, although, while, since |
| 16-18 | Common & proper nouns, abstract nouns | courage, freedom, happiness + capitalization |
| 19-21 | Pronoun review (subject/object/possessive) | I/me/my, he/him/his, they/them/their |
| 22-23 | Articles & demonstratives review | a/an/the, this/that/these/those |
| 24-25 | Unit 1 Review: mixed skills | Combines all reviewed skills |

**Vocab themes:** Character traits, school life, nature
