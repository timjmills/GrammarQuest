# Pre-K ELL Curriculum Outline — Grammar Quest

## Overview
- **150 lessons** (1 sentence per day)
- **6 units** × 25 days each
- **Each lesson**: 5 corrections, full POS tagging, manipulation task, vocabulary word
- **Target**: Pre-K English Language Learners (ages 3-5) at international schools
- **Design basis**: TPR, comprehensible input, receptive-before-productive, spiral review, thematic vocabulary clusters

## Design Principles for Pre-K ELL
- **Receptive before productive**: Units 1-2 emphasize listening/comprehension; production increases in Units 3-6
- **Concrete & visual**: Only tangible, picturable nouns and physical action verbs
- **Short sentences**: 4-5 words (Units 1-2) → 4-6 words (Units 3-5) → 5-7 words (Unit 6)
- **Massive repetition**: Same high-frequency words recycled across days; 40% spiral review from Unit 3 onward
- **Social/survival language first**: Greetings, classroom commands, needs, family
- **Phonetic CVC errors**: Simple, sound-based misspellings (kat→cat, buk→book, hed→head)
- **Gentler explanations**: Even simpler child-friendly language than Grade K
- **ELL sentence frames**: "I see a ___", "This is ___", "I like ___" woven throughout

## POS Tags Used in Pre-K

| Tag | Label | Examples | Introduced |
|-----|-------|----------|------------|
| N | Noun | cat, dog, mom, book, hand | Unit 1 |
| V | Verb | run, sit, see, like, clap | Unit 1 |
| ART | Article | a, the, my | Unit 3 |
| PREP | Preposition | in, on, at, to | Unit 4 |
| ADJ | Adjective | big, red, soft, happy | Unit 5 |
| PRO | Pronoun | I, he, she, we, it | Unit 6 |

## Correction Types
- **Capitalization**: First word of sentence only (no proper nouns until Unit 5)
- **Spelling**: CVC phonetic errors (kat→cat, brd→bird, hom→home)
- **Grammar**: Very simple (a→the, missing article, teaching points)
- **Punctuation**: Period only in Units 1-4; question mark introduced Unit 5; exclamation Unit 6

## ELL Considerations for Pre-K
- Articles (a, the) introduced explicitly in Unit 3 with sustained practice
- SVO sentence order modeled consistently in every sentence
- Pronouns introduced last (Unit 6) after strong noun foundation
- Sentence frames and starters provided with every vocabulary word
- POS color-coding provides visual scaffolding
- High-frequency sight words embedded in every lesson
- Tier 1 vocabulary chosen for maximum accessibility
- Phonetic CVC spelling errors model sound-letter relationships
- Receptive language emphasized before productive output
- Massive spiral review ensures retention
- Thematic clusters build vocabulary webs

## Vocabulary Strategy
- ~10-12 new core vocab words per unit, ~65 total unique core words
- Words drawn from: Dolch pre-primer, basic ELL survival vocabulary, thematic clusters
- Each vocab entry uses the same format as Grade K (def, simple, examples, similar, antonyms, nonex, example sentence, starter, why)
- All vocab words are Tier 1 concrete words appropriate for 3-5 year olds
- Vocabulary spirals across units for reinforcement
