# Grade 5 Grammar Quest - 150-Day Curriculum Outline

## Overview
150 lessons across 6 units (25 days each), covering all Grade 5 CCSS Language standards with strategic review of Grade 3-4 foundational skills. Designed for ELL learners at an international school.

---

## UNIT 1: Foundations & Review (Days 1-25)
**Theme:** "Building Strong Foundations"
**Focus:** Strategic review of Grade 3-4 skills + Grade 5 introductions

### Learning Objectives
- Review irregular plurals, possessive nouns, and abstract nouns (Grade 3 review)
- Review progressive tenses and modal verbs (Grade 4 review)
- Review prepositional phrases and relative pronouns (Grade 4 review)
- Introduce perfect tense concept (present perfect: have/has + past participle)
- Introduce correlative conjunction awareness (either/or, both/and)
- Review comma rules: series, compound sentences, dialogue

### Daily Skill Focus
| Days | Skill Focus | Standards |
|------|------------|-----------|
| 1-3 | Irregular plurals + abstract nouns review | L.3.1b, L.3.1c |
| 4-6 | Simple, progressive, and modal verb review | L.4.1b, L.4.1c |
| 7-9 | Relative pronouns + prepositional phrases review | L.4.1a, L.4.1e |
| 10-12 | Adjective order + comparative/superlative review | L.4.1d, L.5 adj |
| 13-15 | Present perfect tense introduction (have/has walked) | L.5.1b |
| 16-18 | Subject-verb agreement with compound subjects | L.5 usage |
| 19-21 | Frequently confused words (to/too/two, their/there/they're) | L.4.1g |
| 22-23 | Correlative conjunctions intro (either/or, both/and) | L.5.1e |
| 24-25 | Unit 1 Review: mixed skills integration | All Unit 1 |

### Vocabulary Theme: Academic foundations (describe, explain, identify, observe, compare)
### Error Types: spelling, capitalization, grammar, punctuation (balanced)

## UNIT 2: Perfect Tenses & Tense Mastery (Days 26-50)
**Theme:** "Time Travelers"
**Focus:** All three perfect tenses + tense shift correction

### Learning Objectives
- Master present perfect tense (have/has + past participle)
- Master past perfect tense (had + past participle)
- Master future perfect tense (will have + past participle)
- Recognize and correct inappropriate tense shifts
- Use verb tense to convey sequences, states, and conditions
- Distinguish between simple, progressive, and perfect tenses

### Daily Skill Focus
| Days | Skill Focus | Standards |
|------|------------|-----------|
| 26-28 | Present perfect deep practice (have/has + PP) | L.5.1b |
| 29-31 | Past perfect introduction and practice (had + PP) | L.5.1b |
| 32-34 | Future perfect introduction and practice (will have + PP) | L.5.1b |
| 35-37 | Irregular past participles (eaten, driven, written, spoken) | L.5.1b |
| 38-40 | Tense shift identification and correction | L.5.1d |
| 41-43 | Tense choice for sequence and condition | L.5.1c |
| 44-46 | Perfect vs. simple vs. progressive comparison | L.5.1b,c |
| 47-48 | Mixed tense mastery with complex sentences | L.5.1b,c,d |
| 49-50 | Unit 2 Review: tense integration | All Unit 2 |

### Vocabulary Theme: Time and sequence (previously, subsequently, meanwhile, eventually, prior)
### Error Types: heavy grammar (tense errors), spelling, punctuation

## UNIT 3: Conjunctions, Clauses & Sentence Architecture (Days 51-75)
**Theme:** "Master Builders"
**Focus:** Correlative conjunctions, clauses, complex sentences, interjections

### Learning Objectives
- Master all correlative conjunction pairs (either/or, neither/nor, not only/but also, both/and, whether/or)
- Identify independent and dependent clauses
- Classify sentences as simple, compound, or complex
- Use subordinating conjunctions to form complex sentences
- Expand, combine, and reduce sentences for style
- Introduce interjections

### Daily Skill Focus
| Days | Skill Focus | Standards |
|------|------------|-----------|
| 51-53 | Correlative conjunctions: either/or, neither/nor | L.5.1e |
| 54-56 | Correlative conjunctions: not only/but also, both/and, whether/or | L.5.1e |
| 57-59 | Independent clauses identification | L.5 sentence |
| 60-62 | Dependent clauses with subordinating conjunctions | L.5 sentence, TEKS |
| 63-65 | Simple vs. compound vs. complex sentence classification | L.5 sentence |
| 66-68 | Expanding and combining sentences | L.5.3a |
| 69-71 | Reducing sentences and varying style | L.5.3a |
| 72-73 | Interjections introduction (Wow! Oh! Hey!) | L.5.1a |
| 74-75 | Unit 3 Review: sentence architecture mastery | All Unit 3 |

### Vocabulary Theme: Connection and structure (furthermore, consequently, nevertheless, whereas, although)
### Error Types: grammar (conjunction/clause errors), punctuation (comma usage), spelling

## UNIT 4: Advanced Punctuation & Mechanics (Days 76-100)
**Theme:** "Precision Editors"
**Focus:** Introductory elements, direct address, titles, dialogue, advanced commas

### Learning Objectives
- Use commas after introductory elements
- Use commas for direct address, "yes"/"no," and tag questions
- Format titles using italics/underlining/quotation marks
- Punctuate items in a series including phrases and clauses
- Master dialogue punctuation with quotation marks
- Capitalize abbreviations, initials, acronyms, and organizations

### Daily Skill Focus

|------|------------|-----------|

| 85-87 | Commas for "yes"/"no" and tag questions | L.5.2c |
| 88-90 | Titles: italics/underlining for books, movies, plays | L.5.2d |
| 91-93 | Titles: quotation marks for poems, stories, songs | L.5.2d |
| 94-96 | Series of phrases and clauses with commas | L.5.2a |
| 97-98 | Abbreviations, initials, acronyms capitalization | TEKS G5 |
| 99-100 | Unit 4 Review: punctuation mastery | All Unit 4 |

### Vocabulary Theme: Precision and editing (revise, clarify, elaborate, precise, concise)
### Error Types: heavy punctuation, capitalization, spelling, grammar

## UNIT 5: Word Roots, Figurative Language & Advanced Vocabulary (Days 101-125)
**Theme:** "Word Detectives"
**Focus:** Greek/Latin roots, figurative language, context clues, academic vocabulary

### Learning Objectives
- Use Greek and Latin roots/affixes to determine word meanings
- Interpret similes and metaphors in context
- Recognize and explain common idioms, adages, and proverbs
- Use context clues (cause/effect, comparisons) for meaning
- Understand word relationships (synonyms, antonyms, homographs)
- Master consonant changes in related words (select/selection, music/musician)
- Use academic vocabulary signaling logical relationships (however, although, moreover)

### Daily Skill Focus
| Days | Skill Focus | Standards |
|------|------------|-----------|
| 101-103 | Greek roots: photo, graph, auto, tele | L.5.4b |
| 104-106 | Latin roots: port, ject, spect, dict | L.5.4b |
| 107-109 | Prefixes: de-, em-/en-, anti-, ex-, multi-, trans- | L.5.4b |
| 110-112 | Suffixes: -tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ive, -able/-ible | L.5.4b |
| 113-115 | Similes and metaphors in context | L.5.5a |
| 116-118 | Idioms, adages, and proverbs | L.5.5b |
| 119-121 | Context clues: cause/effect and comparison | L.5.4a |
| 122-123 | Consonant changes: select/selection, music/musician | TEKS G5 |
| 124-125 | Unit 5 Review: word study integration | All Unit 5 |

### Vocabulary Theme: Word origins and figurative meaning (derive, metaphor, literal, figurative, etymology)
### Error Types: spelling (root-based words), grammar, punctuation, capitalization

## UNIT 6: Mastery & Integration (Days 126-150)
**Theme:** "Grammar Champions"
**Focus:** Comprehensive skill integration, dialect/register awareness, culminating review

### Learning Objectives
- Integrate all perfect tenses in complex, multi-clause sentences
- Apply all punctuation rules in sophisticated writing contexts
- Use correlative conjunctions in compound-complex sentences
- Compare varieties of English (dialects, registers) in literature
- Demonstrate mastery of all Grade 5 parts of speech
- Combine expanding, reducing, and varying sentence structures
- Apply all vocabulary strategies independently

### Daily Skill Focus
| Days | Skill Focus | Standards |
|------|------------|-----------|
| 126-128 | Integration: perfect tenses + complex sentences | L.5.1b,c + sentence |
| 129-131 | Integration: all conjunction types + clause work | L.5.1a,e + sentence |
| 132-134 | Integration: advanced punctuation in complex writing | L.5.2a,b,c,d |
| 135-137 | Dialects and registers in stories and poems | L.5.3b |
| 138-140 | Integration: all POS identification in complex text | L.5.1a |
| 141-143 | Integration: vocabulary strategies + figurative language | L.5.4,5 |
| 144-146 | Sentence combining, expanding, reducing mastery | L.5.3a |
| 147-148 | Comprehensive review: all Grade 5 standards | All standards |
| 149-150 | Culminating mastery: Grade 5 celebration | All standards |

### Vocabulary Theme: Achievement and growth (accomplish, persevere, proficient, eloquent, culminate)
### Error Types: balanced mix of all types at high complexity

## Cross-Cutting ELL Supports (Every Lesson)
- Vocabulary entries include ELL-friendly "simple words" definition
- Sentence starters for oral/written practice
- Visual supports through color-coded POS
- Sentence frames in manipulation tasks
- Academic vocabulary with logical relationship signals (however, although, nevertheless, moreover, in addition)
