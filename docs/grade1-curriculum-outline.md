# Grade 1 Curriculum Outline — Grammar Quest

## Overview
- **150 lessons** (1 sentence per day)
- **6 units** × 25 days each
- **Each lesson**: 5 corrections, full POS tagging, manipulation task, vocabulary word
- **Target**: Grade 1 students (ages 6-7), including English Language Learners

## ELL Considerations
- Articles (a/an/the) receive explicit, sustained instruction starting Unit 1
- SVO sentence order is modeled consistently
- Verb tenses are introduced sequentially (present → past → future)
- Sentence frames and starters provided with every vocabulary word
- Visual supports through POS color-coding in the app
- High-frequency sight words used throughout
- Tier 2 vocabulary words chosen for accessibility and utility

## Sentence Design Guidelines
- **Length**: 6-8 words (Unit 1) growing to 8-12 words (Unit 6)
- **Vocabulary**: High-frequency sight words + targeted Tier 2 words
- **Errors**: Exactly 5 per sentence (capitalization, spelling, punctuation, grammar mix)
- **POS**: Every word tagged — no word skipped
- **Spelling errors**: Age-appropriate progression (CVC → digraphs → blends → VCe → vowel teams → r-controlled)
- **Manipulation**: Simple swaps (change noun, change adjective, change verb tense)
