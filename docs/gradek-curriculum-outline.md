# Grade K (Kindergarten) Curriculum Outline — Grammar Quest

## Overview
- **150 lessons** (1 sentence per day)
- **6 units** x 25 days each
- **Each lesson**: 5 corrections, full POS tagging, manipulation task, vocabulary word
- **Target**: Kindergarten students (ages 5-6), including English Language Learners
- **Standards basis**: CCSS L.K.1-L.K.6, IXL ELA, TEKS K, cross-referenced with WIDA ELD

## Design Principles for Kindergarten
- **Decodable sentences**: Built from CVC words + high-frequency sight words
- **Gradual length increase**: 5-7 words (Units 1-2) → 6-8 words (Units 3-4) → 6-9 words (Units 5-6)
- **Phonetic spelling errors**: Age-appropriate misspellings based on sound-letter relationships
- **Every word tagged**: No word is skipped in POS identification
- **Familiar contexts**: School, home, family, pets, play, food, nature

## POS Tags Used in Grade K

| Tag | Label | Examples | Introduced |
|-----|-------|----------|------------|
| N | Noun | cat, dog, mom, school | Unit 1 |
| V | Verb | run, sit, jump, is, are | Unit 1 |
| ART | Article | a, the | Unit 2 |
| PREP | Preposition | in, on, at, to, with, by, under, over | Unit 2 |
| ADJ | Adjective | big, red, soft, two | Unit 3 |
| PRO | Pronoun | I, he, she, we, they, it | Unit 4 |
| POSS | Possessive | my, his, her | Unit 4 |

## Correction Types
- **Capitalization**: First word of sentence, pronoun "I", names of people/pets
- **Spelling**: Phonetic CVC errors (kat→cat, dg→dog), sight word errors (iz→is, wuz→was)
- **Grammar**: Article use, verb agreement, pronoun selection
- **Punctuation**: Period (.), question mark (?), exclamation point (!)

## ELL Considerations for Kindergarten
- Articles (a, the) introduced explicitly in Unit 2 with sustained practice
- SVO sentence order modeled consistently in every sentence
- Pronouns introduced with visual support (pictures of he/she/they)
- Sentence frames and starters provided with every vocabulary word
- POS color-coding provides visual scaffolding
- High-frequency sight words embedded in every lesson
- Tier 2 vocabulary chosen for accessibility with simple, child-friendly definitions
- Phonetic spelling errors model the sound-letter relationship learning process
